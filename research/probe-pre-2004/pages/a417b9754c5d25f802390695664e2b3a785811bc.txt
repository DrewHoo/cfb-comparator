Georgia, 17-14
ATHENS, Ga. (AP) — Sophomore Kevin Butler booted a 44-yard field goal with 1:11 remaining to cap a fourth quarter rally and lift the sixth-ranked Georgia Bulldogs to a 17-14 college football victory over Brigham Young Saturday.
The winning kick was set up when the Bulldogs, 2-0, marched 40 yards in the last three minutes, keyed by a 23-yard burst by Herschel Walker to the Cougars' 33-yard line.
Walker, who rushed for 124 yards on 31 carries, had gotten Georgia even at 14-14 with only 5:36 left when he dove over for a 1-yard touchdown.
