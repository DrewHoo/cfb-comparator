# FBS player factoids: known-answer gate corpus

Method: the backbone is the NCAA FBS record book. The current edition (records through the 2025 season) is at `https://s3.amazonaws.com/fs.ncaa.org/Docs/stats/football_records/FBS.pdf`, and the 2022 edition is at `http://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/stats/football_records/2022/FBS.pdf`. I extracted the text of both with macOS PDFKit and saved it under `pages/` as `<sha1(url)>.txt`. Every seed was checked against the record book's Individual Records and All-Time Leaders tables. Players outside the top lists got a Wikipedia page as the receipt. The scope sentences come from Wikipedia, SI and ESPN: regular season against bowls, the Fiesta Bowl split, and a through-six-games line. Those pages are saved as raw HTML under `pages/`. Every quote is a verbatim line or table row, and `node scripts/check-receipts.mjs --pages research/factoids-players/pages --offline research/factoids-players/factoids.json` passes all 127 receipts. I fetched no Sports-Reference pages.

Counts: 65 rows (56 record-book, 8 wikipedia, 1 article; by window: 46 season, 12 career, 4 single game, 1 first-N-games, 1 fastest-to-M, 1 consecutive-games window), 6 rejections, 11 tried, 6 leads, 6 unverified.

The three definition disputes most likely to bite the gates:

1. **Sacks inside rushing.** NCAA rushing is net of sack yardage. Graham Harrell's 2007 rushing is -91 and Kyler Murray's 2018 rushing is 1,001, one yard over the 1,000 mark. If the box-score rushing does not count sacks as rushes, every quarterback's rushing, total offense and "1,000/4,000" club line will drift. The rows `harrell-2007-negative-rushing` and `kyler-murray-2018` are the tripwires.
2. **Postseason inclusion and the counting rule behind "record".** NCAA season and career totals include every postseason game since 2002, and the CFP brings the count up to 16 games. Brennan's 58 touchdown passes in 2006 were 53 in the regular season. Jeanty's 2,601 was 2,497 before the Fiesta Bowl. Pumphrey's career record and Sanders' 2,628 exist only because pre-2002 bowls are excluded. A regular-season toggle has to reproduce both numbers.
3. **What the box score cannot hold.** Several publisher numbers depend on fields a box score does not carry: vacated stats (Reggie Bush 2005 is still in the box scores), FCS seasons (Cam Ward's 158 is 71 FCS plus 87 FBS), starts and QB wins (Bo Nix 61 against Gabriel 63; Kellen Moore's 50–3 printed next to 52 starts), position (QB rushing records), and class year (freshman records). Small source disagreements fall here too: Zappe's completions are 475 per NCAA and 476 per Wikipedia, and Wikipedia says Zappe tied Burrow's 65 touchdowns responsible for while NCAA lists Burrow alone.
